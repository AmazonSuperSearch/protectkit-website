Windows Security and System Protection

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 8, 2026

ProtectKit respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the ProtectKit Windows application, ProtectKit website, referral program, and related services.

ProtectKit is designed primarily to analyze and manage security information locally on your Windows computer. Certain optional online features require information to be transmitted to ProtectKit services or third-party services.

1. Information ProtectKit May Process

Depending on the features you use, ProtectKit may process:

  • Email address: An email address may be used for account, licensing, referral, or related service functionality.
  • Device identifier: ProtectKit may use a device identifier for purposes such as identifying referral registrations, validating referrals, preventing duplicate or fraudulent referral activity, and retrieving referral reward status.
  • Referral information: If you participate in the ProtectKit referral program, ProtectKit may process referral codes, referral account identifiers, referred-user information, referral status, and earned ProtectKit Premium rewards.
  • Information you voluntarily provide: If you contact ProtectKit for support, feedback, bug reports, or other inquiries, information you provide may be processed in order to respond to your request.

2. How Information Is Used

Information processed by ProtectKit may be used to:

  • Provide and operate ProtectKit features.
  • Manage ProtectKit accounts and licensing.
  • Operate and verify the ProtectKit referral program.
  • Calculate and provide referral rewards.
  • Prevent duplicate, fraudulent, or abusive referral activity.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of ProtectKit and related services.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Local Security Information

ProtectKit performs security and system analysis on Windows computers. This can include examining information related to Windows security settings, Microsoft Defender, Windows Firewall, network connections, services, startup programs, DNS configuration, system configuration, and other security-related information.

Much of this functionality is performed locally on the user's computer.

Some optional investigation features may communicate with external services when the user chooses to use those features. Information sent to an external service is subject to that service's applicable privacy practices.

4. Referral Program

ProtectKit includes an optional referral program. When the referral system is used, ProtectKit may transmit information including an email address, device identifier, referral code, referral identifier, and information necessary to determine referral eligibility and rewards to ProtectKit's referral service.

This information is used to register referral participants, associate referrals with users, verify qualifying referrals, prevent abuse, and provide applicable ProtectKit Premium rewards.

5. Third-Party Services

ProtectKit may integrate with third-party services for functionality such as payment processing, licensing, security investigation, reputation analysis, or other optional online features.

When a user chooses to use functionality provided by a third party, information necessary to perform the requested service may be transmitted to that provider. Third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Payments

Payments for ProtectKit Premium may be processed by third-party payment providers. ProtectKit does not directly process or store complete payment card information when payments are handled by those providers. Payment information is handled according to the privacy and security practices of the applicable payment provider.

7. Data Sharing

ProtectKit does not sell personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate ProtectKit, provide a feature requested by the user, process transactions, maintain security, prevent fraud or abuse, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

8. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information processed by ProtectKit and its associated services. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

Information associated with online ProtectKit services may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the applicable service, maintain security and fraud-prevention records, satisfy legitimate operational requirements, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

10. Children's Privacy

ProtectKit is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If ProtectKit becomes aware that personal information from a child has been collected in circumstances requiring parental consent and such consent has not been obtained, reasonable steps will be taken as required by applicable law.

11. Your Privacy Choices

Users may choose not to use optional online features such as the ProtectKit referral program.

Depending on applicable law and the information involved, users may have rights regarding access, correction, or deletion of their personal information. Privacy-related requests may be submitted using the contact information below.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as ProtectKit evolves, new features are introduced, or legal and operational requirements change. When material changes are made, the updated policy will be published with a revised effective date.
