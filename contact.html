A little context can make troubleshooting much easier

You do not need to be technical. Describe what you were trying to do, which ProtectKit page or feature was involved, what you expected to happen, and what actually happened.

For technical problems

Include the exact error message when possible. Screenshots and relevant ProtectKit logs can also help, but review them before sending because they may contain system information.
