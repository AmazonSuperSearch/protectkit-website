Promotion Guidelines

Keep ProtectKit promotions accurate and trustworthy

Do

Describe features accurately, disclose your affiliate relationship, use your approved tracking link, and explain ProtectKit in your own voice.

Avoid unsupported claims

Do not claim ProtectKit guarantees protection, detects every threat, prevents every attack, or replaces all other Windows security protections.

Do not use fear-based deception

Do not tell users their computer is infected, hacked, monitored, or unsafe unless you have legitimate evidence supporting that statement.

Keep pricing current

Current ProtectKit Premium pricing is $3.99/month or $29.99/year. Check the official ProtectKit site before publishing time-sensitive pricing.

Protect the brand

Do not imply that Microsoft, Windows, or another company endorses your promotion unless that endorsement actually exists.

Ask for help

If you need product information, marketing support, or a dedicated affiliate landing page, contact ProtectKit before publishing questionable claims.
